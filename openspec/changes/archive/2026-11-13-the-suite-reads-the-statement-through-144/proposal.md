# The suite reads the statement through 144

## Why

boomdrivedev/blueprints a8234b3 adds clause 144 to timber's statement:
every publish has a link, a claim timber signs about the version's
index naming the index and the landing before it. It follows 20 in
A.3. The commit also adds the vocabulary's link and the setups of C.17,
and answers section 10's last question; Parts A and B change nowhere
else against 90cf023.

The suite reads the statement at 90cf023 and holds 1 to 143, so 144 is
in neither the list nor the register, and no run reports it waiting
(core.33).

## What Changes

- The suite reads the statement at `boomdrivedev/blueprints a8234b3`
  and holds 1 to 144, 144 after 20 in A.3
  (`crates/timber-conformance/src/statement.rs`).
- The register names 144 with phase 2 and its reason
  (`crates/timber-conformance/src/register.rs`): a publish signs each
  output's provenance and the publisher's layout policy about its
  index, and no link; a link takes the form the family's tools share
  (weir 114), which weir's statement spells in no form yet; and no unit

  which the planner replaces with the unit it carves, in phase 1 or 2.
  Naming a unit of phase 1 here would name one the plan does not hold,
  which core.33 rules out.
- `docs/architecture/_open.md` names publish as code that stands
  against 144.
